AI BREAST CANCER CARE

"Leveraging Deep Learning for Early and Accurate Detection of Breast Cancer from Mammograms."

Problem Statement

Breast cancer is a leading cause of mortality among women worldwide. Early detection is crucial for survival, but manual interpretation of mammograms is time-consuming and prone to human error. There is a critical need for automated systems to assist radiologists in identifying potential abnormalities with high accuracy and speed.

LITERATURE REVIEW

Analysis of existing CAD systems reveals limitations in false positive reduction and localization accuracy.

INNOVATION

Integrates dual-view analysis (CC & MLO) with ResNet architecture for enhanced risk stratification.
